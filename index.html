How it works

You have unused power

Your GPU, Mac or AI workstation can spend 60% of the day doing nothing. Atomic matches it with AI demand, and you earn from every request it completes.

You run an AI model

Choose a model that fits your device. It runs when you are not using your hardware, and people pay to use it.

People use the model

A prompt goes to Atomic Nodes. The network sends the request to a model like yours, then returns the answer to the user.

You get paid

You keep 100% of the inference reward from every completed request. During alpha, an eligible node can also earn uptime rewards 24/7 while it stays online.

What is ATOMIC?

ATOMIC is the reward unit for the Atomic Node network. Your node earns it for completed requests and eligible uptime during alpha. The calculator shows ATOMIC first. Its USD figure is an illustration, not a live market price.

FAQ

What does my device run?

You choose a supported AI model that fits your device. The model runs on your device through the node app.

Can I pause the node?

Yes. Pause it whenever you need your device back. New requests stop before the node goes offline.

What am I paid for?

You earn ATOMIC for completed requests. During alpha, eligible verified nodes can also earn uptime rewards while online.

How is estimated revenue calculated?

The calculator uses your hardware speed and the listed model price for continuous 24/7 output. It converts that estimate to ATOMIC using the displayed illustrative rate, not a live market price. Real earnings depend on demand.

Do I need to buy a new device?

No. Start with a supported device you already own.
