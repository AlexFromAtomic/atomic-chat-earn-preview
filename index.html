Why people trust us

Your machine does the work without ever learning who asked for it, and you can read the code that makes that true.

FAQ

What your device runs, when you can pause it, and how your earnings are counted.

What does my device run?

Atomic assigns one supported model that fits your verified memory and measured speed. The model runs locally through the node agent.

Can I pause the node?

Yes. You choose the work window and can pause at any time. New requests stop routing before the node pauses.

What am I paid for?

Verified availability and completed inference are recorded separately. Live demand, model price, measured speed and uptime determine the final rate.

How is estimated revenue calculated?

The comparison uses output speed × 24 hours × the lowest listed OpenRouter output price. Atomic takes 0%. Actual earnings depend on network demand.

Do I need to buy a new device?

No. Start with a supported device you already own.
