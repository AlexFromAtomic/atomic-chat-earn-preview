Prompts stay protected as they move through the network, and anyone can check the open-source code.

  • Open-source code

How it works

You have unused power

Your hardware can sit unused for 60% of the day. Put that time to work and earn passive income.

You run an AI model

Choose a model that fits your device. It runs when you are not using your hardware, and people pay to use it.

People use the model

A prompt goes to Atomic Nodes. The network sends the request to a model like yours, then returns the answer to the user.

You get paid

You earn for every request your hardware completes and keep 100% of the reward. During alpha, you also earn uptime rewards while your node stays online.

FAQ

What does my device run?

You choose a supported AI model that fits your device. The model runs on your device through the node app.

Can I pause the node?

Yes. Pause it whenever you need your device back. New requests stop before the node goes offline.

What am I paid for?

You earn for completed requests. During alpha, you also earn uptime rewards while your verified node stays online.

How is estimated revenue calculated?

The calculator uses your hardware speed and the listed model price for continuous 24/7 output. It shows a maximum, not expected earnings. Real earnings depend on demand.

Do I need to buy a new device?

No. Start with a supported device you already own.
